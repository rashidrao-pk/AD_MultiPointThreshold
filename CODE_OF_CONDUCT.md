# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to create an open, respectful, and collaborative environment for researchers, developers, and contributors.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Welcome feedback and discussion
- Focus on technical and scientific improvement
- Support collaborative research

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Offensive comments
- Personal attacks
- Spam or malicious behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove inappropriate contributions or comments.

---

This project follows principles inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
